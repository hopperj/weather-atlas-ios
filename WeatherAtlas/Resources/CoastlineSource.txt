The data is shipped in the app as static cartographic artwork. The app makes no
Natural Earth network requests and performs no collection or geographic ETL.
MapKit decodes the existing lines for display above the server's weather imagery.
These generalised regional-scale lines fade out between map zooms 7.5 and 8.5
to avoid implying street-level accuracy. Apple Maps supplies fine-scale geography.
